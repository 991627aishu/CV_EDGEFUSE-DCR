How to Use EDGEFUSE-DCR

8-Step Guided Protocol

Comprehensive user guide and operational protocol for researchers, judges, and evaluators.

01
S1

Choose Your Dataset

Select between NYU Depth V2 (Indoor Kinect), KITTI (Outdoor LiDAR), or Middlebury (Dense Stereo). Each features authentic sensor noise profiles.

02
S2

Explore Scene Images

Click "Inspect in Scene Viewer" on any card to preview full-resolution RGB, input depth, validity masks, and ground truth with side-by-side comparison before reconstruction.

03
S3

Custom Input Upload

Upload custom RGB guidance (.png/.jpg) and depth maps (.npy or 16-bit PNG). Ordinary color photographs are scientifically detected and rejected to protect geometric integrity.

04
S4

Configure Parameters

Select upsampling factor (2×, 4×, 8×), choose algorithm (A0 through A5), and toggle texture-copy suppression to eliminate edge bleeding from flat textures.

05
S5

Execute Reconstruction

Click "Execute Depth Reconstruction". PyTorch model inference executes on CPU and hybrid bilateral filtering refines depth boundaries in real time.

06
S6

Inspect Diagnostics

Switch between 4 visualization tabs: Primary Reconstruction, Ablation Progression (A0 vs A2 vs A5), Edge & Discontinuity maps, and Confidence & Absolute Error maps.

07
S7

Compare Benchmarks

Analyze the CVPR-grade benchmark table: Lower is better for RMSE, MAE, and Latency; Higher is better for PSNR, SSIM, and Boundary F1. Best values receive green badges.

08
S8

Export Results

Download raw metric floating-point arrays (.npy), high-res colorized depth PNGs, learned confidence maps, validity masks, error maps, and complete evaluation JSON reports.

Official Dataset Inventory & Benchmark Archive

Complete catalog of official benchmark datasets supported by EdgeFuse-DCR

NYU Depth V2 Official Benchmark 1,449 Official Scenes

Indoor structural dataset recorded across 3 cities using Microsoft Kinect v1 IR structured-light sensors. Contains 407,024 raw frames and 1,449 densely annotated and inpainted ground-truth pairs across 26 indoor scene categories.

Supported Categories: Living Room, Bedroom, Office, Kitchen, Bathroom, Classroom, Dining Room, Conference.
KITTI Depth Completion Benchmark 85,898 Official Frames

Outdoor autonomous driving benchmark collected via Velodyne HDL-64E rotating LiDAR (64 laser beams, 10 Hz) synchronized with PointGrey high-resolution color stereo cameras across 138 driving sequences. Ground truth accumulated via ICP semi-dense registered point clouds.

Supported Modalities: Road transit, Residential avenues, Commercial intersections, Campus.
Middlebury Stereo 2021 Benchmark 24 Laboratory Sets

Laboratory mobile multi-view stereo setup captured with dual Canon EOS 40D DSLR cameras and structured light pattern projection for subpixel ground truth disparities.

Full 24 Scene Sets: artroom1/2, bandsaw1/2, chess1/2/3, curule1/2/3, ladder1/2, octogons1/2, pendulum1/2, podium1, skates1/2, skiboots1/2/3, traproom1/2.
