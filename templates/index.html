Scientific Motivation

Why Depth Completion Matters

Autonomous driving, robotic manipulation, and augmented reality rely on 3D geometric depth. However, raw physical sensors encounter fundamental mechanical and optical boundaries.

01

Sensor Sparsity Dilemma

Mechanical LiDAR captures only 4–6% of scene pixels. Structured light dropouts occur on specular or dark surfaces, creating extensive voids.

• KITTI: ~95.6% void pixels
• Kinect: Specular dropouts
02

Naive Interpolation Trap

Standard bilinear and bicubic interpolation treat depth as continuous, blurring across object boundaries and causing false "flying pixels".

• Severe depth bleed
• High boundary error (F1: ~0.52)
03

High-Resolution RGB Priors

Dense RGB guidance images provide rich photometric contours that strongly correlate with physical 3D object silhouettes.

• Cross-modal edge guidance
• Photometric gradient priors
04

Confidence-Guided Weighting

Not all measurements are equally reliable. Distance-attenuated confidence weighting assigns high trust to valid seeds while suppressing unmeasured noise.

• Pixel-wise confidence matrix
• Dynamic spatial attenuation
05

Bilateral Discontinuity Solver

Fast bilateral edge refinement enforces smoothness strictly along flat regions, halting depth propagation abruptly at true physical contours.

• Zero depth bleed across silhouettes
• Texture-copy suppression
06

The EdgeFuse-DCR Solution

Unifying a dual-branch CNN with a deterministic bilateral confidence solver delivers state-of-the-art RMSE across indoor and outdoor benchmarks.

• State-of-the-Art RMSE
• Fast CPU-friendly inference
User Workflow

How to Use EDGEFUSE-DCR

Follow this 8-step workflow to explore genuine sensor datasets, test custom inputs, or reproduce CVPR benchmark results.

STEP 01

Select a Dataset

Choose between NYU Depth V2, KITTI LiDAR, or Middlebury 2021.

STEP 02

Pick a Scene

Browse verified scenes using dual thumbnails. Click "Load in Workspace".

STEP 03

Upload Custom Inputs

Upload custom RGB photos and depth maps (.npy / 16-bit PNG).

STEP 04

Configure Calibration

Set physical scale to Millimeters (16-bit), KITTI scale, or Metric Floats.

STEP 05

Reconstruction Setup

Choose upsampling factor (2×, 4×, 8×) and algorithm (A0 through A5).

STEP 06

Execute Pipeline

Click "Execute Depth Reconstruction". PyTorch inference runs asynchronously.

STEP 07

Inspect Diagnostics

Examine Primary, Ablation Progression, Edge Contours, and Error Heatmaps.

STEP 08

Export Results

Download raw .npy arrays, 16-bit PNGs, confidence maps, and JSON reports.
